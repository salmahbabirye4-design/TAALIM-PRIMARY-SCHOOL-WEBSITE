QURAN MEMORIZATION (TAHFIDH)

Learning, Memorizing and Living the Holy Quran with Proper Tajweed

About Our Tahfidh Program

Ta-alim Primary School and Quran Memorization Center offers a structured program where students memorize the Holy Quran under qualified teachers. We emphasize correct Tajweed, discipline, and Islamic character development.

Program Objectives

Memorize the Holy Quran accurately
Apply correct Tajweed rules
Build strong Islamic character
Develop discipline and consistency

Tahfidh Levels

Beginner Level (Juz Amma)
Intermediate Level (Selected Surahs)
Advanced Level (Full Quran Memorization)

Tajweed Training

Makharij (Correct Pronunciation)
Rules of Noon Sakinah & Meem Sakinah
Rules of Madd (Stretching)
Ghunnah and Recitation Fluency

Daily Activities

Morning Quran Memorization
Teacher Supervised Revision
One-on-One Recitation
Group Revision Sessions

Weekly Program

Students are tested weekly to ensure proper memorization and revision. Strong performers are encouraged and guided to higher levels of memorization.

Benefits of Our Tahfidh Program

Students gain strong memory skills, spiritual development, discipline, confidence in recitation, and a deep connection with the Holy Quran.
